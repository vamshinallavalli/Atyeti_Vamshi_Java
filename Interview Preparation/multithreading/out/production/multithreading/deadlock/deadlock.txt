What is a deadlock in multithreading?
A situation where two or more threads are blocked forever, each waiting for the other to release a resource (lock). None can proceed.


What are the 4 necessary conditions for a deadlock? (Coffman conditions)

Mutual Exclusion – At least one resource must be held in non-shareable mode.

Hold and Wait – A thread holds at least one resource and is waiting for another.

No Preemption – Resources cannot be forcibly taken; only voluntarily released.

Circular Wait – A circular chain of threads, each waiting for the next.

Give a simple real-world example of deadlock.

Thread A holds Lock1 and waits for Lock2
Thread B holds Lock2 and waits for Lock1
→ Both wait forever.

How to prevent deadlock? (Top strategies)

Always acquire locks in a fixed global order (e.g., by object hash or ID)
Use tryLock() with timeout (java.util.concurrent.locks)
Avoid nested locks when possible
Use high-level concurrency utilities (ConcurrentHashMap, BlockingQueue, etc.)
Minimize lock scope and duration
Use lock hierarchy and documentation

Quick

Best way to avoid deadlock? → Always lock in the same order.
Can you have deadlock without synchronized keyword? → Yes, with ReentrantLock, semaphores, etc.
Does volatile prevent deadlock? → No.
Does Thread.join() cause deadlock? → Yes, if two threads join each other.
