Mutual Exclusion
Each phone can be used by only one person at a time (you can’t both talk on the same phone simultaneously).

Hold and Wait
Person 1 picks up Phone A and starts using it.
Now he wants to call Person 2, so he starts waiting for Phone B.
At the same time, Person 2 picks up Phone B and starts using it.
Now she wants to call Person 1, so she starts waiting for Phone A.
→ Both are holding one phone and waiting for the other.

No Preemption
Nobody can forcibly grab the phone from the other person’s hand.
The phone will be released only when the holder decides to hang up voluntarily.

Circular Wait
Person 1 is waiting for Phone B (which is held by Person 2)
Person 2 is waiting for Phone A (which is held by Person 1)
→ A perfect circle: 1 → 2 → 1


Result: Deadlock!
